## **Project Overview**

### 1. **Game Summary**
- Local multiplayer (same device) gameplay.

### 2. **Gameplay Mechanics**
- Players place bombs to reduce the opponent's health to zero. The last player standing wins.

### 3. **Controls**
- **Player 1**:  
  - Move: `W`, `A`, `S`, `D`  
  - Place Bomb: `Spacebar`
- **Player 2**:  
  - Move: Arrow keys (`↑`, `↓`, `←`, `→`)  
  - Place Bomb: `Left Ctrl`

### 4. **Character List**
